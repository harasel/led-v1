Canberra case study

IRT Kangara Waters

Five apartment buildings where the controls, not just the fittings, changed the equation.

Challenge

Common areas across five apartment buildings — 74 lights in each — needed lighting available around the clock. Running every fitting at full output all night meant the buildings were paying for light long after anybody was using it.

Solution

LED Solutions combined efficient fittings with occupancy control and dimming. When an area is vacant, a portion of the lights switch off entirely and the remainder drop to a low background level. Movement returns the area to full output, then it times back down after 30 minutes.

Result

Lighting stays available whenever residents need it, while the hours of unnecessary full-output operation are cut right back.
